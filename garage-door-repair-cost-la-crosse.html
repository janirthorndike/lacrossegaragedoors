Cost questions

How much does garage door repair cost here?

Most common repairs in the La Crosse area: spring replacement about $180–$380, opener repair $100–$200, a new opener installed $350–$500, cables $130–$200, rollers $120–$200, and a tune-up $89–$129, plus a $49–$69 service call that applies toward the repair. You always get a firm quote first.

How much is a broken spring?

Typically $180–$380. A single spring on a smaller door is at the low end; both springs on a double-car door is at the high end. On a two-spring door we recommend doing both at once — see the spring replacement page.

Is there a service call fee?

Yes, $49–$69 to come out and diagnose the problem, and it's applied toward the repair if you go ahead. No extra charge for evenings or weekends.

Why ranges instead of one flat price?

Every door is different — spring type, door weight, opener brand, and the condition of the surrounding parts all move the price. The ranges here are honest typical numbers, and we give you a firm quote for your exact door before any work starts.
